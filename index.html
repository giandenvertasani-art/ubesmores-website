A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Cookie questions?

We've saved you a seat—and a few answers.

Can I mix different flavors?+

Yes! Choose a mixed box of exactly 6 or 8 cookies, then divide it among your favorite flavors. Solo orders contain one flavor in any quantity.

When should I place my order?+

Please order at least one day in advance. Choose tomorrow or a later date in the order form.

Can I choose pickup or delivery?+

Yes. Choose your order method at checkout. For delivery, include your complete address.

How do I keep my cookies cozy?+

Keep them in an airtight container. Warm them briefly before enjoying for a soft, gooey bite.
